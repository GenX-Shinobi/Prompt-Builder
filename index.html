Start here: how this works in four steps
  1. Pick your chatbot (Step 0). Its commands, notes and paste limit load automatically.
  2. Stack commands: Bot Commands holds the switches and typed commands for that chatbot, Prompt Craft works with any of them.
  3. Write your prompt, attach files if you need to, then press Copy & launch. Long prompts split into parts for you.
  4. Keep it fresh: chatbots change often. Each chatbot's note says when it was last checked. A grey chip means something is unverified, in conflict between sources, or has not been checked for 60 days. No chip does not mean confirmed, so check the app when it matters.
